# Introduction

## Build contactless payments into your solution

Halo Dot is a SoftPOS provider that enables banks, merchant acquirers, payment service providers and software businesses to deliver contactless payment acceptance on compatible mobile devices.

Our technology enables you to embed payments into existing applications, connect an application to a payment companion app, or launch a payment application under your own brand. Flexible deployment models allow you to choose how Halo Dot’s technology fits into your infrastructure and operating model.

This documentation guides product and development teams through the available integration and deployment options, with technical guidance to get started.

## Payment acceptance across devices

SoftPOS uses software to enable payment acceptance on compatible devices. Customers pay by tapping a supported contactless card, digital wallet or wearable against the merchant’s device, using NFC.

Halo Dot supports compatible Android smartphones and enterprise devices, as well as supported iPhones through Tap to Pay on iPhone. This allows partners to introduce payment acceptance into everyday business workflows, from retail checkout and hospitality to delivery and field services.

Device, operating-system and market requirements vary by implementation. Refer to the relevant platform guide when planning your solution.

## Integration options

Choose how payment acceptance becomes part of your application and merchant experience.

| Form factor | How it works |
| --- | --- |
| **Halo.SDK: Embedded** | Integrate contactless payment acceptance directly into your own or a partner’s mobile application with our software development kit. |
| **Halo.Link: App to App** | Connect an existing application to a payment companion app. The companion app handles the payment interaction and returns the result to your application. |
| **Halo.Plus: White Label** | Launch a branded payment application using Halo Dot’s white-label solution, with features and customisation suited to your requirements. |

Tap to Pay on iPhone has its own platform requirements and integration guidance.

## Deployment models

The deployment model determines how Halo Dot connects to your payment infrastructure and how operational responsibilities are shared.

| Deployment model | How it works |
| --- | --- |
| **Managed** | Halo Dot hosts and manages the SoftPOS environment, reducing the operational responsibilities your team needs to take on. |
| **Adaptor** | Deploy the Adaptor within your own environment to connect Halo Dot services to your acquiring platform. This gives you control over deployment and operations, with responsibilities shared with Halo Dot. |
| **Core** | Integrate Halo.SDK into your application and connect SoftPOS to your existing payment infrastructure. Encrypted payment data is returned to your application, while you retain control of backend transaction processing and connections. Core does not require an Adaptor deployment. |

The available combination of integration and deployment options depends on your platform and processing requirements.

## Security and compliance

Halo Dot’s SoftPOS offering includes PCI MPoC Software and Solution listings. Halo Dot is also an Apple-approved Gateway Service Provider (GSP) for Tap to Pay on iPhone.

Security capabilities include device integrity checks, attestation, payment-data encryption and secure PIN entry, where applicable to the platform and integration.

Integration, testing and certification requirements vary according to the solution being deployed.

## Tap on Own Device

Halo Dot also supports Tap on Own Device through scheme-approved pilots. These journeys allow consumers to tap a payment card or supported wearable against their own compatible device.

This extends contactless technology into online payments and other customer interactions involving a card. Availability is subject to the relevant scheme approval and the scope of the pilot.
